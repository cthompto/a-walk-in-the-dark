A Walk in The Dark

By Chelsea Thompto

Instructions:

This piece is composed of six poems each with six lines, the poems are displayed across a variety of repeating spaces. When one poem ends another is randomly chosen. Move through the poems using your keyboard:

  • Use the "W" key or Up Arrow to move forward.
  • Use the "S" key or Down Arrow to move backward.

Project Overview
